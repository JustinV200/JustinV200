## 🚀 Metrify.fit

I founded and built Metrify from scratch, a full-stack iOS and Android fitness app that swaps the usual dropdown-menu food and workout logging for just talking to it. Tell it what you ate or how the lift went and it handles the rest.

The AI coach runs on RAG and deterministic tool calls over a vectorized database instead of letting the model guess at numbers, and it's holding up at 97.5% accuracy across 2,600+ adversarial test cases. Live on iOS now with 35+ users, Android is next.

Try it at [metrify.fit](https://metrify.fit).
